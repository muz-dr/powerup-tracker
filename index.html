🤖 Gemini AI Nutrition Tracker

Use Google Gemini to automatically estimate your daily nutrition from photos or text descriptions of your meals — then import the result here in one tap.

📋 How to use

1
Open Google Gemini — go to gemini.google.com in your browser or open the Gemini app. Make sure you are on the Gemini 1.5 Pro model or higher for best accuracy.
2
Copy and send the prompt below — tap Copy Prompt, paste it into Gemini, and send it. This sets Gemini up to track your meals for the day.
3
Log each meal as you eat — send a photo of your food, or describe it in text (e.g. "2 fried eggs, 1 cup white rice, grilled chicken 150g"). Gemini will keep a running tally.
4
At end of day, type "Final Summary" — Gemini will reply with a JSON block containing your totals for calories, protein, carbs, fat and sodium.
5
Tap 🤖 Gemini Fill above — paste the JSON into the popup. Your nutrition form will fill automatically. Review, then tap Log Day.
💡 Pro tip — use AI Mode Search: In the Gemini app, you can enable Search integration so Gemini can look up nutritional data for branded foods and restaurant meals in real time, giving you much more accurate values than standard estimates.

📋 Prompt to copy

I will send you photos or descriptions of my breakfast, lunch, dinner and snacks throughout the day. Track each meal as I send it, keeping a running total. When I say "Final Summary", give me the day's totals strictly inside a JSON code block using exactly this format so I can paste it into my nutrition tracker: ```json { "calories": [Total], "protein": [Total], "carbs": [Total], "fat": [Total], "sodium": [Total] } ``` Use whole numbers only. Do not include units inside the values. Start tracking now.
